AgRaz Privacy Policy, Data Security & Ownership

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · Effective for the AgRaz mobile app and website

At AgRaz (operated by AGRAZ Solutions LLP), we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our mobile and web platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the AgRaz app, we may collect information needed to deliver agricultural services and improve your experience, including:

  • Personal information (name, phone number, location, farm size, language preference)
  • Usage data (interactions with app features, time spent, login patterns, device type)
  • Agricultural data (crop types, expenses, forecasts, trading records, financial inputs)
  • Service registration details if you list a business or service on AgRaz

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information collected to provide and enhance AgRaz services:

  • Deliver features such as crop forecasting and yield prediction
  • Provide market price analysis and farming expense management
  • Connect you with relevant buyers, sellers, vendors, or service providers
  • Send alerts, reminders, product suggestions, or educational content
  • Operate account authentication, support, and app security

AgRaz does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data with trusted partners only when required to deliver specific services, under confidentiality obligations.

3. Data Security and Protection

We take data protection seriously and use industry-standard measures, including:

  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS / SSL/TLS)
  • Access controls and monitoring for backend systems
  • Regular reviews to identify and fix potential vulnerabilities

While no digital platform can guarantee 100% security, AgRaz follows best practices to minimize risks and safeguard your data.

4. Ownership of Information and Intellectual Property

Content, features, source code, design, algorithms, and models used within AgRaz are the intellectual property of AgRaz / AGRAZ Solutions LLP. You retain ownership of your personal and farm-related data. By using our app, you grant AgRaz a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to process and analyze this data to provide services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain user data for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You can request data deletion by emailing privacy@agraz.in or agraz.solutions@gmail.com, or by using in-app support.

6. Third-Party Services

AgRaz may integrate with third-party services (for example payment gateways or maps). Those services operate under their own privacy policies. AgRaz is not responsible for the data practices of third-party platforms outside our control.

7. Your Rights

You have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your information
  • Correct or update inaccurate details
  • Delete your data from our system
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

AgRaz is intended for farmers and agribusiness users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of AgRaz after changes means you accept the updated policy.
